# Fit Test Australia: homepage redesign concept

A homepage-only redesign concept for Fit Test Australia (FTA), built as a single static page (`index.html`) with local assets. No build step and no framework: open `index.html` or serve the folder.

## What is on the page

Hero, FTA Shop product slider, services, why choose FTA, trust figures and client logos, industries, pricing and preparation, clinic locator with live maps, blog, final call to action and footer. It also has a WhatsApp button, a back-to-top button, scroll animations and a responsive layout.

## Sources and items to verify before publication

- Business information, figures, prices, product details, addresses, blog posts, client logos and accreditation statements are taken from the live site (fta.net.au) and need FTA's confirmation.
- Service, product, technician, blog and logo images come from fta.net.au and are stored in `assets/img/`.
- Hero and industry photography is placeholder stock from Unsplash (free licence). Replace it with FTA's own on-site photography.
- The logo is FTA's existing artwork recoloured to the new brand blue (`assets/fta-logo.svg`, `assets/fta-logo-white.svg`).
- The WhatsApp button uses 0440 138 105. Confirm this number is on WhatsApp.
- The page is marked `noindex` so the concept does not compete with the real site in search results.

## Brand tokens

Navy `#10263D`, brand blue `#2A66AE`, soft blue `#EAF1FA`, background `#F2F5F9`. Headings use Manrope and body text uses Inter.
