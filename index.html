Before Your Test

Come prepared.

  • Be clean shaven on the day. No beards or goatees.
  • No smoking, vaping or food for 30 minutes before the test.
  • Bring PPE that is compatible with your respirator.

A passing test takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

How often? When a respirator is first issued, at least once a year, and whenever you are issued a new size, style, make or model, as set out in AS/NZS 1715:2009.
